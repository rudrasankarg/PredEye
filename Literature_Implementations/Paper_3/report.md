# Paper 3: Thrivaad - Multilingual Predictive Eye-Sign-Based AAC

## Dataset
- **Name:** Custom Eye Signs Dataset
- **Size:** Thousands of video frames of eye signs/gestures
- **Details:** Cropped eye regions, brightness augmentations.

## Algorithms & Models
- **Model:** Optimized CNN with Word Prediction module
- **Task:** Eye-sign classification and next-word prediction.

## Implementation Details
- **Optimization Technique:** Adam optimizer
- **Regularization:** Batch Normalization, Dropout.
- **Metrics:** Accuracy, F1 Score.
- **Training:** Trained on local languages eye-sign dataset.

## Results
- **Accuracy:** ~91.8%
- **F1 Score:** ~0.90
